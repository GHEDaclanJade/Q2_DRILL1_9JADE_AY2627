Beginner's Guide to Volleyball 🏐

📜 Basic Rules

  • Game finishes when one team scores 25 points
  • There are 6 players on each side
  • You can't catch or hold the volleyball
  • You can't touch the ball twice

🎒 Volleyball Equipment

  • A volleyball
  • Knee pads
  • Sports shoes (for volleyball or basketball is fine)
  • Finger tape

🏃 Basic Techniques

  • Bump
  • Set
  • Spike
  • Block
